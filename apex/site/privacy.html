2What we collect and why

The Paddock (free). When you sign up with the form on apexcode.uk, we collect your email address. We store it in a Notion database and in our members system, which runs on a server in London (DigitalOcean). We use it to send you the welcome email, the Bank Builder guide and the weekly log email every Sunday. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Paying members. When you subscribe through Stripe checkout, we receive your name and email address, your Stripe customer and subscription IDs, and your subscription status (for example active, paused or cancelled). We use these to give you your membership, manage billing, let you into the members area and email you about your account. Stripe processes your card. Your card details never reach us. Stripe's own privacy policy applies to what Stripe does.

Signing in to the members area. You sign in to members.apexcode.uk with a one-time link sent to your email address. We don't store passwords, because there aren't any. Once you're signed in, we set one strictly necessary session cookie to keep you signed in (see section 5).

Anonymous page-view counts. On apexcode.uk we count page views per day. For each view we record only the page path, the domain of the website that linked to it (if any), and any utm_source, utm_medium or utm_campaign tags in the link. We don't use cookies for this, we don't store IP addresses, and we don't build profiles of anyone. These counts can't be used to identify you.

Sending email. Our emails are sent from hello@apexcode.uk through our website host's mail server (SiteGround).

Telegram. Our free channel at t.me/TheApexCodeUK runs on Telegram, a third-party platform. Joining it is optional. Telegram handles your data under its own privacy policy.

3Who else handles your data

We use a small number of service providers. They process data for us under their own terms and data protection commitments.

  • Stripe: payments and subscription billing.
  • Notion: stores The Paddock email list.
  • DigitalOcean: hosts our members system on a server in London.
  • SiteGround: hosts the website and its mail server, which sends our emails.
  • Telegram: runs the free channel, if you choose to join it.

Some of these providers are based outside the UK or may process data outside the UK. Where that happens, they rely on safeguards recognised under UK data protection law.

We don't sell your data. We don't share it with anyone for marketing. We would only disclose it otherwise if the law required us to.

4Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct anything that's wrong;
  • delete your data (subject to records we must keep for tax and accounting);
  • object to how we use it, including where we rely on legitimate interests;
  • portability: get your data in a common, machine-readable format.

Where we rely on consent (The Paddock emails), you can withdraw it at any time. The quickest way is the unsubscribe link in any email.

To use any of these rights, email hello@apexcode.uk. It's free. We may need to confirm it's really you before we act.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

5Cookies

  • apexcode.uk sets no cookies. Not for analytics, not for advertising, not for anything.
  • members.apexcode.uk sets one cookie: a strictly necessary session cookie, set after you sign in with your one-time link, so you stay signed in. It lasts 30 days, or until you sign out. It isn't used for tracking or advertising. Because it's strictly necessary, it doesn't need your consent, but without it you can't use the members area.

We use no trackers, advertising pixels or third-party analytics.

6Automated decisions

We don't make any decisions about you by automated means, and we don't profile you.
