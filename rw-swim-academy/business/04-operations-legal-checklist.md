## 11. Setup sequence: weeks 1 to 4 (from w/c 28 September 2026)

### Week 1 (w/c 28 Sept): decide and apply for the slow things

- Accountant hour: structure, VAT question, bookkeeping choice (Chris and Ruby)
- Companies House name check; register the Ltd (Chris)
- DBS application started via the governing body or umbrella body; or confirm the existing check and Update Service status (Ruby)
- Book NRASTC and first-aid courses for the earliest dates (Ruby)
- Locate the qualification certificate; confirm exact award title (Ruby)
- Join Swim England or STA as an individual member (Ruby)
- Pool hire enquiry emails to at least five venues (Chris, template `06` (j))
- Buy the domain; open the business email; claim the three social handles (Chris and Ruby)

### Week 2 (w/c 5 Oct): money and insurance

- Open the business bank account (Chris and Ruby)
- Insurance quotes from both routes; buy once the qualification and DBS details are to hand (Chris)
- Stripe account in the trading entity's name; Payment Links for each offer in `02` (Chris)
- Bookkeeping software connected to the bank (Chris)
- Follow up every venue by phone; arrange visits (Chris)
- Safeguarding course completed online (Ruby); awareness course (Chris)

### Week 3 (w/c 12 Oct): paper

- Terms and conditions, cancellation policy, privacy notice, safeguarding statement and complaints procedure adapted from `06` and sent for a solicitor or insurer check (Chris)
- Booking, medical and consent form built and tested with one friendly family (Chris)
- Venue visits; collect NOP and EAP; write the RW risk assessment for each shortlisted venue (Chris and Ruby)
- ICO registration (Chris)
- Google Business Profile created and verification started (Chris)
- Level assessment sheet and taster script finalised (Ruby)

### Week 4 (w/c 19 Oct): sign and rehearse

- Sign the first venue agreement(s); pay any deposit (Chris)
- Insurance certificate, DBS and qualification copies sent to the venue (Chris)
- Dry run: one full taster with a family friend's child, using the real form, the real sheet and the real follow-up message (Ruby and Chris)
- Compliance folder audit against the "8 things" box at the top of this file (Chris)
- Go / no-go for tasters from w/c 26 October (Chris and Ruby)

If any of the eight items in the box is not ticked by the end of week 4, tasters move a week. They do not start with a gap.
