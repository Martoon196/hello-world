---

## 1. Mode and framing

**Mode:** Ecosystem build. The brief already has a core offer (1:1 blocks) and a set of surrounding offers. The job here is to make each one do a specific job, to make the bridges between them obvious, and to check the whole ladder against the objections real Sevenoaks parents will raise.

**The problem being solved:** a parent who has never heard of Ruby needs a low-risk first step, a clear next step, a reason to stay term after term, and a reason to tell a friend. Every offer below exists to do one of those four things. Anything that does not is cut.

**Assumptions I am working from (from the brief):**

- Audience: parents in Sevenoaks, Tonbridge, Tunbridge Wells, Westerham, Otford and Kemsing; 60% "confidence parents" of 4 to 10 year olds, 30% "club-track parents" of 8 to 14 year olds, 10% pairs and adults.
- Delivery: Ruby alone, hired lanes with a venue lifeguard, Mon to Fri 4 to 7 pm and Sat 8 am to 1 pm, more in holidays.
- Constraint: 6 free tasters a week, and Ruby's training comes first.
- Brand voice: warm, straight-talking, no hype; "swimmers" never "pupils".

## 2. Audience read: what each parent is actually buying

| | Confidence parent | Club-track parent | Pair / adult |
|---|---|---|---|
| Surface want | "Get my child swimming properly" | "Fix her breaststroke kick / get him a PB" | "Two kids, one slot, less money" |
| Deeper want | Stop the Saturday-morning dread; see the child proud of themselves; be able to say "she can swim" | Confirmation the child is on a path, from someone who has walked it | Value, convenience, both children happy |
| What they tried | Two or more terms of council group lessons; maybe a private teacher who never wrote anything down | Club sessions with 1 coach to 20 swimmers; YouTube technique videos | Separate lessons at different times |
| Why it did not work | Ten children, one teacher, no individual attention, no written progress | No 1:1 time; no video; coach cannot single them out | Cost and diary |
| The must-have | Warmth plus visible, written progress | Technical credibility plus a plan they can take back to club | Price per swimmer |
| The objection we must kill first | "What if my child doesn't take to her?" | "Is an 18-year-old really qualified to coach my club swimmer?" | "Will two get less than one?" |

The confidence parent buys on **trust and proof of progress**. The club-track parent buys on **credibility and technical specificity**. The whole ladder is built to earn trust cheaply first, then prove progress in writing.

## 4. Each offer in detail

### Offer 0: Free Taster & Assessment

- **Name:** Free Taster & Assessment
- **Positioning line:** Twenty minutes in the water with Ruby. A written level for your child. No obligation.
- **Hero summary:** Before you spend a penny, Ruby gets your child in the pool for 20 minutes, then spends 5 minutes with you on poolside explaining exactly where they are and what she would work on first. You leave with a one-page written assessment, whatever you decide next.
- **Headline option:** "Find out where your child really is in the water. Free, in 20 minutes."
- **Who it is for:** Any child aged 4 to 14 whose parent is undecided. Especially confidence parents burnt by group lessons.
- **What is included:** 20 min 1:1 in the water; 5 min parent conversation; written level assessment (Splash / Stroke / Sprint sheet from `06`); a recommendation of one specific next offer; a founding-member place held for 48 hours if available.
- **Price and logic:** £0. The cost to us is one lane slot (about £12 to £18 at £25 to £45/hr **[CONFIRM]**). If half of tasters convert to a £195 block, cost per acquired swimmer is about £35, which is cheap. The cap of 6 a week protects paid slots and keeps the offer scarce in a way that is true.
- **Risk reversal:** It is free, and the child's assessment is theirs to keep. The risk is on us.
- **Upsell path:** Ruby recommends one thing, in writing, on the sheet: "Recommended: 1:1 block of 6, Tuesdays 4.30" or "Recommended: Splash Programme, January intake". Chris follows up within 24 hours with the Stripe link. Not a menu; one recommendation.
- **The objection it kills:** "What if my child doesn't take to her, and I've paid £195?"
- **Rules to keep it honest:** parent present; booked via the form (medical and consent collected before, not on poolside); no more than 6 a week; if the slot is not used it is offered to the waiting list.

> **Options: free taster or £15 credited taster?**
>
> | | A. Free (as brief) | B. £15, credited against the first block | C. Free for the first 90 days, then £15 credited |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | Bookings | Highest | Fewer, but almost all attend | High at launch, filtered later |
> | No-shows | Higher (expect 15%) | Very low | Managed |
> | Trust signal to confidence parents | Strongest | Slightly transactional | Strongest when it matters most |
> | Admin | Waiting list needed for no-shows | Refund logic if they do not book | Two settings to explain |
> | Recommendation | **Yes, for launch.** The first 30 are about trust and momentum. Cap it at 6, require the form, and text a reminder the evening before. | Not yet | **Yes, as the review point at 30 swimmers.** Once there is a waiting list, the credited taster stops the taster being a free lesson for people who were never going to buy. |

### Offer 1: Private 1:1 Lesson

- **Name:** Private 1:1 Lessons
- **Positioning line:** Thirty minutes, one child, one coach, written progress every block.
- **Hero summary:** Every lesson is built around your child: where they are, what is holding them back, what is next. You watch from poolside, you get a short progress note at week 4 and a written report at the end of the block, and Ruby keeps your slot term after term.
- **Headline option:** "Two terms of group lessons and no progress? Thirty minutes of Ruby's attention changes that."
- **Who it is for:** Nervous or plateaued 4 to 10 year olds (confidence parents); children needing to pass school swimming; club-track children between clinics.
- **What is included:** 30 min 1:1; level assessment on file; week 4 progress note (WhatsApp or email); end-of-block written report; same slot held for renewal; parent on poolside; one free date move per block with 24 hours' notice.
- **Price and logic:** £35 single, £195 block of 6 (£32.50), £372 block of 12 (£31). The single exists to make the block look sensible, not to be sold. Comparators a parent reaches for: council group lessons at roughly £7 to £10 per 30-minute lesson (ten children, so roughly £70 to £100 of teacher time per lesson), and private teachers in the area at £30 to £45 per half hour **[CONFIRM local rates]**. £32.50 sits in the middle of private, and the written progress is the difference.
- **Risk reversal:** The brief's guarantee, word for word: "If your child doesn't want to come back after the first lesson of a block, tell us and we'll refund the rest. No awkward conversation."
- **Upsell and cross-sell path:** Block of 6 to block of 12 on renewal; 1:1 to the Progress Programme when Ruby judges the child ready for a group (cheaper per lesson for the parent, better economics for us); Holiday Intensive before school swimming or summer; sibling into a 2:1 pair.
- **The objection it kills:** "Group lessons haven't worked for two terms."

### Offer 2: Semi-Private 2:1

- **Name:** Semi-Private 2:1
- **Positioning line:** Two swimmers, similar level, one coach. Half the price, none of the waiting around.
- **Hero summary:** Siblings or friends at a similar level share a 30-minute lesson. Ruby works them in turns and together, so each gets coaching every minute. One slot in your diary, one payment, two written progress notes.
- **Headline option:** "Two children, one slot, £25 each."
- **Who it is for:** Siblings; school friends; two children from the same class in a Programme who want more.
- **What is included:** 30 min for two swimmers; both assessed; both get progress notes; same renewal rules as 1:1.
- **Price and logic:** £25 per swimmer (£50 per lesson); block of 6 £270 for the pair (£22.50 per swimmer per lesson). The pair pays 43% more than a single 1:1 for the slot, so the lane earns more, and each family pays 31% less per child. Everyone wins, which is why it is easy to sell.
- **Risk reversal:** Same guarantee. Plus: if the two swimmers turn out to be at different levels, Ruby will say so in the first lesson and offer to split them into 1:1 slots with the block value carried across.
- **Upsell path:** Pair to Programme (both into the same group); one of the pair to 1:1 when they diverge.
- **The objection it kills:** "Two children in lessons is too expensive."
- **Rule:** both must be within one level of each other on the assessment sheet. Ruby decides, kindly and in writing.

### Offer 3: RW Progress Programme (Splash / Stroke / Sprint)

- **Name:** RW Progress Programme
- **Positioning line:** Eight weeks, four swimmers, one level, one goal, written down and ticked off.
- **Hero summary:** A small group of no more than four at the same level, same day and time for eight weeks. Every swimmer starts with an assessment, works through a set of milestones for their level, gets a progress note at week 4, and finishes with a written report and a certificate. Three levels: Splash (water confidence, 4 to 6), Stroke (technique, 6 to 10), Sprint (club-ready, 9 to 14).
- **Headline option:** "Small group. Same four children every week. Progress you can see on paper by week 8."
- **Who it is for:** Confidence parents who want structure and a price below 1:1; Sprint level for club-track parents whose child is not yet in a club; children moving out of 1:1 who now need to swim alongside others.
- **What is included:** 8 x 45 min; max 4; assessment; milestone map for the level; week 4 note; week 8 report and certificate; priority place in the next level's intake.
- **Price and logic:** £160 per swimmer (£20 per 45-minute session). Per swimmer it undercuts 1:1 clearly; per session the lane earns £80 with four swimmers, which is our best format (see `03`). The "product" framing (levels, milestones, certificate) is what makes a group of four feel like more than a cheaper lesson.
- **Risk reversal:** The first-lesson guarantee applies. Plus a run guarantee: if a group does not reach three swimmers, we tell you two weeks before the start and refund in full or move you into 1:1 at the block rate.
- **Upsell path:** Splash to Stroke to Sprint; Programme plus a Holiday Intensive to "jump a level"; Sprint to Stroke Clinic; a 1:1 top-up for a swimmer who needs one skill fixed.
- **The objection it kills:** "1:1 is more than I want to spend, but I don't want to go back to a group of ten with no plan."
- **Rules:** minimum 3 to run; one level per group, no exceptions; parents told the 8 dates at booking.

### Offer 4: Holiday Intensive

- **Name:** Holiday Intensive
- **Positioning line:** Five days in a row. The fastest progress your child will make all year.
- **Hero summary:** Same time every day, Monday to Friday, with Ruby. Daily repetition does in one week what weekly lessons take a term to do. Ideal before school swimming starts, before a summer holiday, or to break a plateau. 1:1, or a group of four at one level.
- **Headline option:** "Five days. One goal. Ready for the summer."
- **Who it is for:** Children with a deadline (school swimming, holiday, club trial); families who cannot do term-time slots; the January "new year confidence" crowd at February half term.
- **What is included:** 5 consecutive daily sessions (30 min 1:1, or 45 min in a group of four); assessment on day 1; written report on day 5; a "keep it going" recommendation for term time.
- **Price and logic:** £160 1:1 (£32 per session, matching the block rate so it never undercuts term-time blocks) or £95 per swimmer in a group of four (£19 per session). Sells out because holiday pool time is limited and Ruby's holiday availability is wider. Cash arrives before the term-time block cycle, which smooths cash flow.
- **Risk reversal:** Day 1 guarantee: if the child does not want to come back after day 1, refund days 2 to 5.
- **Upsell path:** Intensive to term-time block (offer the parent their preferred slot on day 5, before anyone else); group intensive to Programme.
- **The objection it kills:** "We can't commit to a weekly slot" and "We need a result by a date."

### Offer 5: Stroke Clinic (club swimmers)

- **Name:** Stroke Clinic
- **Positioning line:** One hour, one stroke, filmed and fixed by a swimmer who races it.
- **Hero summary:** Sixty minutes 1:1 with Ruby on one stroke. Above and underwater video, a plain-English breakdown of what is costing time, and a take-home drill plan the swimmer can use at club. For swimmers already in a club, or trialling for one.
- **Headline option:** "Your club coach has twenty swimmers. For an hour, your child has Ruby."
- **Who it is for:** Club-track parents of 8 to 14 year olds; triathletes and adult improvers later.
- **What is included:** 60 min 1:1; video (with consent); written drill plan within 48 hours; a follow-up message after two weeks asking how the drills are going.
- **Price and logic:** £55 single; 3-clinic pack £150 (£50 each). Comparators are 1:1 stroke sessions from club coaches at £40 to £70 per hour **[CONFIRM]** and online video analysis services at £30 to £60. The credibility of the person doing the analysis is the whole pitch. Note from `03`: in a hired lane at £35/hr or more, the clinic's margin is thin; it is a credibility and lead-in offer at this price, not the profit engine the brief's "high-margin" label suggests, unless the venue cost is lower.
- **Risk reversal:** If the drill plan is not with you within 48 hours, the clinic is free.
- **Upsell path:** Single to 3-pack (one stroke each, or one stroke over three sessions); pack to a termly "check-in" clinic; a younger sibling into a Programme; clinics for the swimmer's friends at club (referral offer).
- **The objection it kills:** "My club swimmer has plateaued and the coach can't give them individual time."
- **Rule:** never scheduled against the swimmer's own club training nights; Ruby speaks to the relevant head coach before clinics are advertised to that club's swimmers.

> **Options: stroke clinic format (recommendation only; price stays £55)**
>
> | | A. 60 min in water (as brief) | B. 45 min in water plus 15 min poolside video review together | C. Clinics only at venues with hire under £30/hr, or at the swimmer's own pool where permitted |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | Parent experience | Long, thorough | Parent and swimmer see the video together; feels more expert | Same |
> | Lane cost at £35/hr | £35 | £26.25 | Under £30 |
> | Contribution at £55 | About £18 | About £27 | About £24 or more |
> | Recommendation | Launch with A because it is what the brief promises | **Move to B after the first five clinics if parents rate the review highly**, which they usually do. It is a better product and better economics without touching the price. | Do both B and C where possible |

### Offer 6: Founding Members (first 30 swimmers)

- **Name:** RW Founding 30
- **Positioning line:** The first thirty swimmers get Ruby's best rate for as long as they stay.
- **Hero summary:** The first 30 swimmers to book a block or programme become Founding Members: 10% off their blocks for as long as they keep swimming with us, a free RW swim cap, and first choice of slots every term before anyone else.
- **Headline option:** "Thirty places. Then it's the waiting list."
- **Who it is for:** Everyone who books before the 30th swimmer.
- **What is included:** 10% off the first block **[CONFIRM: the brief says "10% off first block for life", which reads two ways. Chris to confirm which is meant; the wording on the website must match]**; free RW swim cap (about £6 each); priority booking window each term (48 hours before general release).
- **Price and logic:** The discount costs about £3.25 per 1:1 lesson on the block rate. On 30 swimmers that is roughly £1,000 a year if all stay, which is cheap for a guaranteed first cohort and for the urgency of a real, visible countdown ("22 of 30 places taken").
- **Risk reversal:** Same guarantee as the underlying offer.
- **Upsell path:** Founding Members are the referral engine (section 6) and the first to be offered Programmes and Intensives.
- **The objection it kills:** "Why now, rather than next term when you've got reviews?"
- **Rule:** count publicly and honestly. When it is 30, it is closed. Do not reopen it under another name.

## 6. Referral: refer a friend, both get a free lesson

- **The offer:** When a swimmer you referred completes their first block (or Programme), you both get a free lesson: one extra 30-minute 1:1 added to the end of your current or next block, and one added to theirs.
- **Why "completes their first block" and not "books a taster":** it pays for a customer, not a lead. Two free lessons (about £35 of lane time in total) for a £195 block is a good trade.
- **How it works:** every booking confirmation carries a personal line: "Know someone who'd love Ruby? Give them this link. When they finish their first block, you both get a free lesson." Chris tracks it on the booking sheet (referred by: name).
- **Rules:** unlimited referrals; free lessons are added to a block, not paid in cash, and are taken within the following block; Founding Members can refer from day one.

## 10. Bridges between offers (the paths a parent walks)

## 11. Audit (honest, 1 to 5)

| Category | Score | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Audience specificity | 5 | Three clearly drawn parent types with the offer they land on; Sevenoaks and the villages named; ages named |
| Value clarity | 4 | Small numbers plus written progress is easy to grasp. The Programme's "product" framing needs the milestone maps to exist (RW Method, `01` section 6) or it is just a small group |
| Mechanism (why this works when the alternative did not) | 4 | Four or fewer swimmers, assessment on file, week 4 and week 8 notes, a coach who has raced. The mechanism is credible. It would be a 5 with a named framework on the website ("the RW Method: assess, milestone, report") |
| Risk reversal fit | 5 | The first-lesson refund kills the exact fear a confidence parent has. It is cheap to honour (one lane slot) and believable because it is specific |
| Price relative to outcome | 4 | £32.50 a lesson against "she can finally swim" is small. The Stroke Clinic at £55 is under-priced for its credibility and thin on margin in a hired lane; leave it for launch, review with the format option above |
| Urgency, legitimately | 5 | Founding 30 is a real cap; 6 tasters a week is a real cap; Programmes have real start dates; Intensives have real pool time limits. No manufactured countdowns |
| Ecosystem fit and bridges | 4 | Every offer leads somewhere. The weak bridge is Intensive to term block (holiday customers often disappear); the day-5 slot offer addresses it. Adults and triathletes are priced but have no bridge yet, which is fine for launch |

**Category test:** the parent must not see "another swim school". The website and the taster sheet should say "private lessons" and "small groups" and show the written assessment. It passes as long as nothing looks like a leisure-centre timetable.

**Proportionate-response test:** a Sevenoaks mum whose 7-year-old has done two terms in a group of ten with no badge, reading "free 20-minute assessment with a written level, six a week, first 30 swimmers get 10% for life and a refund if the child doesn't want to come back" should say "obvious, book it". It passes.

**Where it could fail:** if lane hire is refused at after-school times, the whole ladder still works but the calendar shifts to Saturdays and holidays, which lowers capacity for 1:1 and pushes the mix towards Programmes and Intensives. That is not a worse business; it is a different timetable. See `03`.
