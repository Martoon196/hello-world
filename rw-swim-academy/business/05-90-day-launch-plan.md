# 05. 90-Day Launch Plan: RW Swim Academy

**For:** Chris, Ruby and whoever helps (a "helper" is a family member or friend doing 2 to 3 hours a week of admin or flyering)
**Prepared:** 26 September 2026
**Runs:** w/c 28 September 2026 to w/c 21 December 2026 (13 weeks), then the January restart
**School dates used:** Kent state school October half term w/c 26 October 2026; autumn term ends around Friday 18 December 2026; spring term starts around Monday 4 January 2027 **[CONFIRM all against Kent County Council's published 2026/27 term dates; independent schools differ]**

**How to run it:** every Sunday evening, 20 minutes, Chris and Ruby: tick last week, read next week, update the KPI sheet (section 6). If a week slips, slip the week, not the standard.

---

## The four phases at a glance

| Phase | Weeks | Dates | Aim | Exit test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Set-up | 1 to 3 | 28 Sept to 18 Oct | Everything legal, financial and practical in place; venue shortlist to signed agreement | "8 things" box in `04` ticked by end of week 4 |
| B. Pre-sell and waiting list | 4 to 6 | 19 Oct to 8 Nov | Build a list of 60 plus interested families; run the first tasters in half term | 40 plus on the interest list; 12 plus tasters done; taster to paid conversion measured |
| C. Founding-member launch | 7 to 9 | 9 to 29 Nov | Sell the Founding 30; fill the first dated blocks and January Programme groups | 20 plus paid swimmers by 29 Nov |
| D. Deliver and collect proof | 10 to 13 | 30 Nov to 27 Dec | First blocks running; assessments, notes, reviews, referrals, vouchers | 30 paid by mid-January; 5 plus reviews; renewal process rehearsed |
