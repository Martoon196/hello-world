## (d) Photo and Video Consent statement

**RW Swim Academy: Photo and Video Consent**

We sometimes film during lessons. There are two reasons: to show a swimmer and parent what is happening under the water (coaching feedback), and, with your permission, to show other families what lessons with Ruby are like.

**What we promise:**
- We never photograph or film a child without the written consent you give on the booking form.
- We never post a child's face, name or school unless you have ticked "yes" to each of those separately.
- Coaching footage is stored on a locked device and a private cloud folder, is shared only with you, and is deleted within 6 months of the lesson unless you have consented to its wider use.
- We will not use images of a child in swimwear in any way that could be considered inappropriate; most of our public images are taken from poolside at a distance, from behind, or show Ruby alone.
- We follow our safeguarding policy and the venue's photography rules at all times. At venues that prohibit photography, we do not film.
- You can change your mind at any time. Email hello@rwswimacademy.co.uk and we will remove the image from anything we control within 7 days. We cannot recall copies others have made once posted, which is one reason we ask you to think carefully.

**Other people's children:** please do not photograph or film other children at the pool. Your own child, from poolside, is fine if the venue allows it.

Consent choices are recorded on the booking form: coaching footage only / social media with face / social media without face / first name with image.

## (k) Complaints procedure

**RW Swim Academy: Complaints Procedure**

We would rather hear about a problem than lose you quietly. This is how to raise one, and what we will do.

**Stage 1: tell us**
Speak to Ruby on poolside after a lesson, or message or email Chris Waller at hello@rwswimacademy.co.uk or [phone]. Tell us what happened and what you would like us to do. We will acknowledge within 2 working days and aim to resolve it within 7 working days. Most things are fixed at this stage: a moved lesson, a credit, a change of approach with your child.

**Stage 2: formal complaint**
If you are not satisfied, email "Formal complaint" to hello@rwswimacademy.co.uk with the details and what you have already been told. Chris Waller will review it, may ask to speak to you, and will reply in writing within 14 days with a decision and reasons. If the complaint is about Chris, Ruby will review it with an independent person.

**Safeguarding concerns**
A concern about a child's welfare is not a complaint and is handled under our safeguarding policy straight away. Contact our safeguarding lead [Chris Waller, phone]. If a child is in immediate danger, call 999. You can also contact the NSPCC helpline or Kent's children's social care front door **[CONFIRM current contact]**.

**Still not resolved**
You may raise the matter with our governing body, [Swim England / STA] **[CONFIRM]**, whose complaints and welfare procedures apply to member teachers. You also have your rights under consumer law, and may seek advice from Citizens Advice.

**What we will never do**
Treat you or your child differently for complaining. Every complaint and what we did about it is recorded, and we review them every term to see what needs to change.
