# Win-Back, Review Request, Testimonial Request and Anniversary Emails

**Voice:** Ruby unless marked. Review request is from Chris to keep Ruby out of the "please rate me" ask.
**Tokens:** `{{first_name}}`, `{{child_name}}`, `{{last_lesson_date}}`, `{{programme}}`, `{{booking_link}}`, `{{review_link}}`, `{{slot_day}}`, `{{slot_time}}`, `{{first_lesson_date}}`.

**Assumptions:** Google Business Profile is verified and has a short review link **[CONFIRM]**; anniversary uses a `first_lesson_date` custom field set at A0.

---

## Win-back 1: Lapsed 60 days (day 60 after last lesson)
**Job:** get a reply or a click by making the return easy and naming a real reason to come back. Framework: BAB (before, after, bridge) in miniature. Hook: humanity. Audience: product-aware, warm but drifted.

**Subject line options**
1. (Humanity) it's been a couple of months. how's {{child_name}} getting on?
2. (Curiosity) the thing that happens after 8 weeks off
3. (Self-interest) {{child_name}}'s file is still here
4. (Question) did we lose you, or did life just get busy?
5. (Direct) a slot for {{child_name}}, if you want it

**Preview text:** No pressure. One genuine question and one easy way back.

**Body**

Hi {{first_name}},

It's been about two months since {{child_name}}'s last lesson on {{last_lesson_date}}. No guilt intended; the term runs away with everyone.

One thing worth knowing: the skills stay, but the confidence fades faster than you'd expect. Eight weeks off and the first lesson back is often "remembering", not "learning". That's fine. It just means sooner is easier than later.

{{child_name}}'s written file is still here. Last note in it: [Ruby fills one line from the week-8 report, e.g. "25 metres front crawl, breathing every three; next is backstroke rotation"]. If they came back, that's where we'd start.

Two options:
- **Same as before:** a block of six 1:1s or the next {{programme}} group. Current slots: {{booking_link}}
- **Just a look:** one single 1:1 at £35 to see where they are. No block needed.

If something didn't work last time, the day, the price, the venue, anything, reply and tell me. I'd rather fix it than guess.

Ruby

PS: If {{child_name}} has moved on to a club or another teacher, that's brilliant. Reply and tell me and I'll stop emailing about lessons, and I'll mean it when I say well done.

**CTA:** Book (link) or reply.

## Win-back 2: 7 days after win-back 1 (only if no reply or booking)
**Job:** one last, shorter ask, then stop. Framework: direct, one idea. Hook: closure.

**Subject line options**
1. (Direct) last one, then I'll stop
2. (Humanity) no hard feelings, {{first_name}}
3. (Self-interest) {{holiday}} intensive might be the easier way back
4. (Question) one question, then I'll leave it

**Preview text:** Either come back for five days at {{holiday}}, or tell me to stop. Both fine.

**Body**

Hi {{first_name}},

Short one, and the last on this.

If a weekly slot is the problem, the {{holiday}} Intensive might suit better: five days in a row, 30 minutes a day, one goal, done. £160 1:1 or £95 in a group of four. Dates: {{intensive_dates}}. {{booking_link}}

If it's not the right time at all, reply "not now" and I'll stop emailing about lessons. You'll still hear from me once a year on {{child_name}}'s swimming anniversary, because I like sending those, and you can turn that off too.

Thanks for having been part of the first year.

Ruby

PS: The file stays. Whenever you come back, we start where we left off, not from the beginning.

**CTA:** Book intensive, or reply "not now".

## Google review request (from Chris, 3 days after the week-8 report)
**Job:** get an honest Google review. Framework: direct with reciprocity. Hook: help a small local business. Audience: hot, just received a certificate.

**Subject line options**
1. (Humanity) would you write us a review? honest is fine
2. (Self-interest) 2 minutes that help the next Sevenoaks family find Ruby
3. (Direct) a Google review for RW Swim Academy
4. (Curiosity) the thing that helps a swim school more than adverts

**Preview text:** One link, two minutes, no need to be nice if it isn't true.

**Body**

Hi {{first_name}},

Chris here, Ruby's dad and the admin half of RW. **[CONFIRM Chris is Ruby's dad and is happy to say so]**

{{child_name}} finished the block this week and Ruby said the report was one of her favourites to write. I'm hoping you might do one thing for us.

Would you write a Google review? It takes about two minutes:

{{review_link}}

Two things to say up front:
1. **Honest is the point.** If something could have been better, say so. We'd rather fix it than have a page of five stars that don't mean anything.
2. **You don't need to name {{child_name}} or say where lessons are.** "My 7-year-old" is plenty. Please don't mention the venue, for the children's safety.

If you're not sure what to write, parents often mention: what {{child_name}} was like in the water before, what changed, and what Ruby was like with them. That's all anyone searching "swimming lessons Sevenoaks" wants to know.

Reviews are how a small business in Sevenoaks gets found without spending money on adverts. Every one moves us up the list a bit.

Thank you, whichever way you go.

Chris

PS: If you'd rather send feedback to us privately instead, reply to this email. It goes to me and Ruby and nobody else.

**CTA:** Leave a review (one link).

**Wording guidance for the review link page or a follow-up Story:** "If you'd like a hand, here's a shape that works: 1. How old is your child and what were they like in the water before? 2. What changed, in plain words? 3. What's Ruby like to deal with? 4. Would you recommend her, and to whom?"

## Testimonial request (from Ruby, sent to parents who left a 5-star review or replied warmly to A8)
**Job:** get a quote and permission to use it. Framework: direct, reciprocity. Hook: humanity.

**Subject line options**
1. (Humanity) could I quote you? (you'd see it first)
2. (Direct) three questions about {{child_name}}'s lessons
3. (Self-interest) help me explain RW to the next nervous parent
4. (Curiosity) the sentence I'd love to put on the website

**Preview text:** Three quick prompts. Answer one or all. I'll never use it without showing you.

**Body**

Hi {{first_name}},

Your review meant a lot. I'd like to ask one more thing, and it's fine to say no.

Could I use a few of your words on the RW website and social media? Not a form, not a rating. Just three prompts, answer any of them in a sentence or two, reply to this email:

1. **Before:** what was {{child_name}} like in the water before lessons, and what were you worried about?
2. **The moment:** was there a specific lesson or week where something changed?
3. **What you'd say to another parent** who's deciding between RW and a bigger group lesson?

How I'd use it:
- First name only (yours), and "my son" / "my daughter" / "my 7-year-old" rather than {{child_name}}'s name unless you tell me you're happy with it.
- No venue, no times, no photos unless you separately say yes.
- I'll send you the exact wording and where it's going before it goes anywhere. You can change it or pull it at any point.

Thank you. The first year of a business runs on people like you saying "yes, it worked".

Ruby

PS: If you'd rather do it as a 20-second voice note on WhatsApp instead of typing, that's genuinely easier for most people. Send it to [Chris's number] and I'll transcribe it.

**CTA:** Reply with answers.

## Anniversary: "One year swimming with Ruby" (automated, `first_lesson_date` + 365 days, Active and Lapsed)
**Job:** delight, remind of progress, soft renewal if lapsed. Framework: story-led. Hook: milestone.

**Subject line options**
1. (Story) one year ago today, {{child_name}} got in the water
2. (Humanity) happy swimming birthday, {{child_name}}
3. (Curiosity) what {{child_name}} couldn't do a year ago
4. (Self-interest) a year with RW: {{child_name}}'s then and now

**Preview text:** Then, now, and thank you. No ask in this one.

**Body**

Hi {{first_name}},

A year ago today, {{first_lesson_date}}, {{child_name}} had their first lesson with me.

From the file, this is what I wrote that day:
> [Ruby pastes the week-1 assessment lines, e.g. "Sat on the edge for five minutes. Feet in. Wouldn't put face in. Kicked to me on a float, holding on tight."]

And the most recent note:
> [Latest progress line, e.g. "25 metres front crawl, breathing every three. Starting backstroke rotation. Asked if they could 'do a race'."]

Same child. One year. I don't take credit for all of it; a lot of that is you doing the bath-time homework and turning up every {{slot_day}}. But I'm proud of it.

{{status_line}}
**[Active: "See you {{slot_day}} at {{slot_time}}, as ever."]**
**[Lapsed: "If {{child_name}} fancies getting back in the water, the file is still here and we'd pick up from that last line, not from the start. {{booking_link}}. No pressure. I just wanted to mark the day."]**

Happy swimming birthday, {{child_name}}.

Ruby

PS: If you've got a photo from that first week and one from now, I'd love to see them. Just for me, not for posting, unless you say otherwise.

**CTA:** None for Active; soft booking link for Lapsed.
