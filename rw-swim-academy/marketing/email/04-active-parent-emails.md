# Active Parent Emails

Emails for families with a current block or programme. Most are transactional (sent regardless of marketing consent). The week-4 and week-8 notes are written per child by Ruby from the templates. Everything else is automated or a broadcast to the Active segment.

**Voice:** Ruby, first person, unless marked as from Chris (admin and money).
**Tokens:** `{{first_name}}`, `{{child_name}}`, `{{programme}}`, `{{block_type}}`, `{{slot_day}}`, `{{slot_time}}`, `{{venue}}`, `{{start_date}}`, `{{end_date}}`, `{{renewal_link}}`, `{{booking_link}}`, `{{referral_link}}`, `{{intensive_dates}}`.

**Assumptions:** payment is taken at booking; renewals are paid via a link; the WhatsApp Channel carries the same term-date and closure notices in short form. **[CONFIRM]**

## A0: Block confirmation
**Job:** confirm, set the routine, restate the rules once. Transactional.

**Subject line options**
1. (Direct) {{child_name}} is booked: {{slot_day}}s at {{slot_time}} from {{start_date}}
2. (Humanity) welcome to RW, {{child_name}}
3. (Self-interest) your {{block_type}}: dates, venue, the two rules

**Preview text:** Everything you need for the next {{lesson_count}} weeks in one place.

**Body**

Hi {{first_name}},

{{child_name}} is booked. Here's everything in one place. Save this email.

**What:** {{block_type}} **[e.g. Block of 6 1:1 lessons / Stroke Progress Programme (8 weeks)]**
**When:** {{slot_day}}s at {{slot_time}}, starting {{start_date}}, last lesson {{end_date}}
**Where:** {{venue}}. {{venue_notes}}
**Working on first:** [Ruby fills from the assessment: two lines]

**The two rules**
1. Moving a lesson: 24 hours' notice, one move per block. Less than 24 hours and the lesson is lost, because the lane is paid for either way. Same-day illness: message before 8am and we'll try to swap.
2. Changing your mind: if {{child_name}} doesn't want to come back after the first lesson, tell us and we'll refund the rest. No awkward conversation.

**What you'll get from me**
- A short progress note at week 4
- A "keep your slot?" email around week 5 or 6, so the same day and time is yours next block if you want it
- A written progress report and certificate at the end

**Founding member?** {{founding_line}} **[e.g. "Yes. 10% is already applied, your cap is waiting on poolside at lesson one, and you'll get priority booking every term." / blank]**

Any questions, reply here. Chris handles bookings and invoices; I handle everything in the water.

Ruby

PS: Costume on under clothes for lesson one. It buys us five extra minutes.

**CTA:** None (transactional). Calendar file attached if the tool supports it.

## A6: Week-6 renewal ("Ruby's held your slot")
**Job:** renew, same slot, before the block ends. Framework: direct with loss aversion. Automated at day 38 of a 6-week block, day 42 of an 8-week programme.

**Subject line options**
1. (Self-interest) I've held {{slot_day}} {{slot_time}} for {{child_name}}. keep it?
2. (Urgency, true) {{child_name}}'s slot goes to the list on {{release_date}}
3. (Humanity) same day, same time, next block?
4. (Direct) next block for {{child_name}}: one click

**Preview text:** Your slot is yours until {{release_date}}. After that it goes to the waiting list.

**Body**

Hi {{first_name}},

{{child_name}}'s block ends on {{end_date}}. I've held {{slot_day}} at {{slot_time}} for the next one, because routine is half of what makes lessons work.

**Keep the slot:** {{renewal_link}}

Next block, we'd pick up from [Ruby's one line from the week-4 note or a generic "exactly where the week-8 report leaves off"]. Same day, same time, same price as this block **[founding members: with your 10% still applied]**.

If you'd like a different day, or to move to a {{programme}} group, reply and tell me before {{release_date}} and I'll do my best.

The slot stays yours until {{release_date}}. After that it goes to the waiting list, because other families are asking for {{slot_day}} evenings.

Ruby

PS: If you're not renewing, no need to explain. A one-line reply saying "not this time" means I can offer the slot to the next family sooner. Thank you.

**CTA:** Keep the slot (one link).

## Lesson cancelled by us (pool closure)
**Job:** tell them fast, tell them what happens to their lesson, apologise once. Transactional. Send by email and WhatsApp Channel at the same time.

**Subject line options**
1. (Direct) {{slot_day}}'s lesson is cancelled: pool closure
2. (Direct) no lesson today ({{date}}), here's what happens next
3. (Humanity) sorry: {{venue}} is closed today

**Preview text:** Not lost. We'll add it to the end of your block or refund it, your choice.

**Body**

Hi {{first_name}},

{{venue}} has just told us the pool is closed on {{date}} because of {{reason}} **[e.g. "a chemical imbalance" / "a lifeguard shortage" / "a heating fault"]**. So {{child_name}}'s lesson at {{slot_time}} is cancelled. Sorry. It's out of our hands but that doesn't make it less annoying.

**What happens to the lesson:** it is not lost. You choose:
1. We add it to the end of your block (default, no need to reply), or
2. We refund that lesson: reply "refund" and Chris will do it within three working days.

Next lesson is {{next_date}} as normal unless you hear from us.

Ruby

PS: If we hear the closure will last more than one week, we'll email again with alternative venue options.

**CTA:** Reply "refund" only if wanted.

## Late-cancellation reminder (gentle, from Chris, sent after a second late cancellation)
**Job:** hold the policy line kindly. Transactional.

**Subject line options**
1. (Direct) a quick note on moving lessons
2. (Humanity) the 24-hour rule, and why we have it
3. (Direct) about {{child_name}}'s lesson on {{date}}

**Preview text:** Not a telling off. Just how the lane hire works.

**Body**

Hi {{first_name}},

Chris here. Not a telling off, promise. Just a quick note because {{child_name}}'s lesson on {{date}} was cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice, and it's the second time this block.

The reason for the 24-hour rule is simple: the lane is hired and paid for whether Ruby is teaching in it or not, and with only six tasters and a handful of 1:1 slots a week, an empty lane is a family who could have had it.

So, as a reminder of how it works:
- 24 hours' notice: we move the lesson, once per block, no problem.
- Less than 24 hours: the lesson is lost.
- Illness on the day: message before 8am and we'll try to swap it.

We've applied the rule this time, so the lesson on {{date}} counts as used. If there's something going on that makes {{slot_day}} at {{slot_time}} hard to keep, tell us and we'll try to find a slot that fits better.

Chris

PS: Ruby says {{child_name}} was doing really well with [thing] last week and she's looking forward to {{next_date}}.

**CTA:** Reply if the slot needs changing.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
