# Local Outreach Copy

Everything here is for Sevenoaks and the villages within about 20 minutes. Voice is Ruby's unless marked as Chris. **[CONFIRM]** items must be checked before sending. Assumptions: Chris handles school and nursery admin contact; Ruby fronts anything in person or in her own voice.

**Strategy decisions:** awareness stage is unaware to problem-aware for schools and nurseries (they do not know RW exists and are not looking for a swim teacher); the lead angle is "useful to you first, business second". For Facebook groups, problem-aware parents asking for recommendations; angle is proof plus a low-commitment free step. For clubs, positioning is complementary; angle is "we send you better swimmers".

### 3c. Reply to a recommendation request ("Can anyone recommend swimming lessons for a nervous 5-year-old?")

Hi [name]. Swimming teacher here, so take this with that in mind, but two things that help nervous five-year-olds regardless of where you go: get them humming with their face in the water in the bath (humming stops water going up the nose, which is usually the actual fear), and check the goggles seal without the strap (press on, do they stick?). Half of "won't put their face in" is leaking goggles.

If you want small numbers, I teach 1:1 and groups of four in Sevenoaks (RW Swim Academy). Free 20-minute taster and I'll tell you honestly where they are. Happy to chat by message either way, no pressure.

### 3d. Reply to "Any recommendations for stroke coaching for a club swimmer?"

Hi [name]. I swim for Maidstone SC and teach on the side (RW Swim Academy, Sevenoaks). I do 60-minute stroke clinics with video analysis and a take-home drill plan, mainly breaststroke and freestyle, which are my strokes. Most of the club swimmers I see have one specific thing (usually breathing or kick order) that a lane coach hasn't had time to fix. Message me if useful. Otherwise, their club coach is the first person to ask; a good one will tell you exactly what to work on.

### 3e. Reply to a "which leisure centre lessons are best?" thread (no pitch)

Honestly, the teacher matters more than the centre. Ask whoever runs it whether the teacher gets in the water with the younger ones and how many are in the class. Under eight per class and a teacher in the water for the nervous ones is what I'd look for. (I teach privately, so I'm biased towards small numbers, but that's the honest answer.)
