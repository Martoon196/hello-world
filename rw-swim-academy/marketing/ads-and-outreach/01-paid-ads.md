# Paid Ads: Meta Local Test and Google Search Set

**Purpose:** a £5 to £10 a day test to fill the six weekly free tasters when organic and referrals are short, and to learn which angle Sevenoaks parents respond to. Not a scale-up plan. Stage 1 should run mostly on organic, groups and referrals.

**Landing page for everything:** `/taster` (free taster and assessment). One page, one form, no menu. **[ASSUMPTION; see landing page notes below]**

**Strategy decisions (before any copy)**
- **Awareness stage:** problem-aware. Sevenoaks parents know their child is nervous or stuck. Most have not heard of RW. Copy leads with the problem, then the proof (Ruby), then the free, low-commitment step.
- **Lead angle by ad set:** (A) problem-led "stuck in a group of ten", (B) proof-led "she's actually done it", (C) identity-led "a coach your daughter looks up to" for the parents-of-girls insight in the brief.
- **Primary benefit:** visible progress. Supporting: small numbers, safety, someone who has raced. Proof: Ruby's record, the written assessment, the refund rule.
- **Trigger:** low-risk entry (free taster, refund rule) plus true scarcity (six tasters a week). No fake countdowns.
- **Framework:** PAS for A, proof-led AIDA for B, identity-led BAB for C.

## Meta (Facebook and Instagram) test

### Campaign structure
- **Objective:** Leads (instant form) or Traffic to `/taster` with the pixel firing on the booking confirmation. Recommendation: send to `/taster` on the website so people read the page and see Ruby; instant forms get more, worse leads. If the website is late, use an instant form with two qualifying questions (child's age, what you'd like help with).
- **Budget:** £7 a day, one campaign, three ad sets (one per angle), two ads each. Run 14 days minimum before judging. Roughly £100 for the test.
- **Placements:** Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed, Instagram Reels, Stories. Exclude Audience Network and Messenger.
- **Schedule:** all day; check whether 7 to 9pm outperforms after week one.

### Targeting
- **Location:** Sevenoaks, Kent, 10-mile radius. (Takes in Tonbridge, Westerham, Otford, Kemsing, Edenbridge, the edge of Tunbridge Wells and Orpington. Consider trimming to 8 miles if leads come from too far to travel.)
- **Age:** 28 to 50.
- **Gender:** all. Mums do most of the research but excluding dads is unnecessary and Meta's delivery will skew naturally.
- **Detailed targeting:** Parents (all), Parents with early school-age children (6 to 8), Parents with preschoolers (3 to 5). Try one ad set with no interest targeting (Advantage+ audience with location and age only) as a control; at this radius, broad often wins.
- **Exclusions:** anyone who has already submitted the taster form (custom audience from pixel or form).
- **Special ad category:** not required (not housing, credit, employment or politics). Ads that show children require consent as per the brief; the test uses Ruby only.

## Google Ads search set

**Why:** search catches parents who have already decided to find lessons this week. Higher intent than Meta, lower volume. £3 to £5 a day is enough at this radius.

**Campaign settings:** Search only (no Display, no Search Partners). Location: 10 miles around Sevenoaks, "presence" not "interest". Phrase match keywords. Ad schedule: 6am to 10pm. Conversion: taster form submission.

### Keywords (10, phrase match)
1. "swimming lessons sevenoaks"
2. "private swimming lessons sevenoaks"
3. "private swimming lessons kent"
4. "1:1 swimming lessons kent" (also add "one to one swimming lessons kent")
5. "children's swimming lessons sevenoaks"
6. "swimming lessons tonbridge"

## Budget and what good looks like

| Channel | Daily | 30-day spend | Expected leads (taster bookings) | Target CPL | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Meta test | £7 | £210 | 10 to 20 | £10 to £20 | Local, low competition. Above £25 a lead after 14 days: change creative before changing targeting. |
| Google search | £4 | £120 | 5 to 10 | £12 to £25 | Low volume; clicks at this radius are often £1 to £3. Above £30: check search terms report for junk. |
| **Total** | **£11** | **£330** | **15 to 30** | **£11 to £22** | |

**What a good CPL means for RW.** A taster converts to a block at 60% (target). A first block is worth £160 to £372, and a retained family renews several times. So a £20 taster lead is roughly a £12 to £15 cost per paying swimmer. Anything under £30 per taster is fine in Stage 1. Anything over £40 for two consecutive weeks: pause, and go back to groups and referrals.

**Capacity check:** six tasters a week is the cap. If ads are filling all six with two days of spend, cut the budget rather than overbook. The point is full tasters, not the lowest possible cost per lead. **[CONFIRM the six-a-week cap holds during launch or whether Ruby will open more in January]**

**What to measure weekly:** spend, taster bookings by ad set, cost per booking, taster attendance rate, taster-to-block conversion by source (ask "how did you hear about us?" on the form). Kill the worst ad in each set after 14 days, keep the best, write one new challenger.
