## 1. What social is for (and what it is not)

Social has one commercial job in Stage 1: **get 30 founding-member swimmers**. Everything posted should do one of three things:

1. Make a Sevenoaks parent trust Ruby enough to book a free taster.
2. Make a club-track parent believe Ruby knows things their child's current teacher doesn't.
3. Keep existing parents feeling looked after so they renew and refer.

It is not for building a "swim influencer" audience. A 400-follower account full of local parents beats a 40,000-follower account of swimmers in Australia.

## 3. Content pillars

Aim for this mix over any two-week window.

| Pillar | Share | What it looks like | Why it sells |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Ruby the athlete** | 20% | Training clips, race day, Kent Counties, what a set looks like, the honest bits (early alarms, bad sessions). Cross-posted from @rubywaller.swims. | "She's actually done it." Club-track parents buy this. Confidence parents like that their daughter has someone to look up to. |
| **2. Teaching tips** | 25% | 15 to 45 second explainers: face in the water, floating, breaststroke kick, goggles that leak, what to say on poolside. Carousels for parents to save. | Demonstrates she can teach, not just swim. Saves and shares drive reach. |
| **3. Swimmer progress (with consent)** | 20% | Before/after clips, milestone moments, "first width", certificate day. Only ever with written consent, per section 7 below. Pre-launch, use Ruby demonstrating instead. | Visible progress is what ICP 1 buys. This is the proof pillar. |
| **4. Behind the scenes** | 15% | Setting up the lane, the kit bag, writing assessments, the whiteboard, the drive between pools, Chris being the admin. | Warmth and trust. Shows a real small business, not a franchise. |
| **5. Local Sevenoaks** | 10% | Knole Park runs, Bligh's on a Saturday, the school run, Sevenoaks Leisure Centre car park at 4pm, local events. | Local signal. Tells the algorithm and the parent that this is a Sevenoaks business. |
| **6. Offers** | 10% | Free taster, founding members, holiday intensives, stroke clinics, slot releases. Always framed as scarcity that is true (6 tasters a week, 30 founding places). | The ask. Kept to 1 in 10 so the account never feels like an advert. |

Rule of thumb: nine posts that help or humanise, one post that asks.

## 8. Safeguarding rules for posting children

These are non-negotiable and should be written into the booking consent form. They also protect Ruby.

**Consent**
- No image or video of a child's face is posted anywhere without written consent from a parent or carer (tick box at booking, plus a one-line WhatsApp confirmation before the specific post goes live: "Happy for me to post the clip of the first width from Saturday? No name, no venue.").
- Consent can be withdrawn at any time; the post comes down the same day.
- Consent is per swimmer, not per family.

**What we never show**
- Child's full name. First name only if the parent explicitly asks, otherwise "one of our Splash swimmers".
- Venue name, pool signage, timetable boards, or anything that identifies where and when a child is regularly in the water.
- School uniform, school bags, or school logos. Ask children to arrive in a hoodie over uniform or crop the frame.
- Changing rooms, poolside benches with belongings, or other children in the background who have not consented (blur or reframe).
- Swimmers in a way that could be reframed by others: no slow-motion, no close crops of bodies, no "before" shots that could embarrass.

**Preferred approach**
- Film from behind, from the side at water level, or with goggles and cap on. Focus on the stroke, the hand, the kick.
- Use Ruby as the demonstrator for teaching content. She is the product.
- Ask parents to send their own clips if they want their child featured; that puts the choice with them.

**Ruby's own safety**
- No posting live from a venue. Post after leaving.
- Turn off location tagging on all posts.
- Comments filtered for keywords; DMs from unknown adult accounts asking about Ruby personally are not answered from the academy account. Chris monitors.
- Under-18 swimmers in ICP 2 who want to tag Ruby: fine on their own accounts, but the academy does not follow children's accounts. **[CONFIRM with club safeguarding guidance; Swim England Wavepower is the reference]**

## 9. Engagement playbook: local Facebook groups

Sevenoaks parents make buying decisions in groups such as "Sevenoaks Mums", "Sevenoaks Community", "Tonbridge Mums", "Westerham Community" and school-specific groups. **[CONFIRM exact group names and each group's rules on business posts; several only allow businesses on a set day]**

**The three rules**
1. **Be a member first, a business second.** Answer three unrelated questions before posting anything about lessons. Recommend other local businesses. Be useful.
2. **Never cold-post an advert.** Post on the group's business day if it has one. Otherwise, only post when the group admin has agreed, or in reply to a genuine request.
3. **Always answer recommendation requests personally, not with a link dump.** "Swimming lessons for a nervous 5-year-old?" gets a reply from Ruby that gives a useful tip first, then mentions that she teaches and offers a free taster.

**Monthly rhythm per group**
- Week 1: an introduction or update post (business day only)
- Week 2 to 4: reply to recommendation requests, comment on relevant threads, share a genuinely useful tip post (no link) once
- Never more than one self-promotional post per group per month

**Chris's role:** monitor groups for "swimming lessons" keywords using Facebook's search and saved searches, and flag them to Ruby to reply within the hour. Speed wins these threads.

**Tone in groups:** first person, Ruby's voice, no hashtags, no emojis beyond one at most, no "DM me hun". Copy templates are in `ads-and-outreach/02-local-outreach.md`.

**Other local channels**
- Nextdoor: one introduction post, then reply to requests only.
- School WhatsApp groups: never post from the business. Parents who are customers will do it for you; give them a short line they can copy (see referral email in `email/04`).
- Mumsnet Local: one listing, reply to threads if asked.
