# Blog Editorial Plan: December 2026 to May 2027

**Purpose:** rank for local search ("swimming lessons Sevenoaks" and the long tail around it), give parents something worth reading, and feed social and email with material. Every post links to the free taster and to at least one programme page.

**Publishing capacity (assumption):** one post a month, 900 to 1,400 words, written by Ruby with Chris editing. Anything more than that will not be sustained in Stage 1 and a dead blog looks worse than no blog. **[CONFIRM capacity; if Ruby can only manage every six weeks, drop posts 7 to 9 rather than rushing.]**

**Balance target across the six months:** 40% educational, 30% authority (Ruby's competitive experience), 20% engagement, 10% promotional. The six launch posts sit at roughly 50/33/17/0 because the promotional job is done by the taster CTA on every post.

**Voice rules for every post:** first person (Ruby), British spelling, short sentences, no hype, no prohibited words (elite, world-class, guaranteed results, Olympian, cheap, kiddies, pupils, students). "Swimmers" and "children", not "students". "Training towards the Olympic pathway", never "Olympian". No em-dashes.

**Research rule:** no statistic goes in a post unless Ruby or Chris has opened the source. Where a post would benefit from a figure, the draft says **[CONFIRM: source]** rather than inventing one. Observational language ("in my experience", "most children I've taught") is fine and honest.

## Structure every post follows

1. **Title** with the keyword near the front, under 65 characters where possible.
2. **Meta description** under 155 characters, includes keyword and "Sevenoaks" or "Kent".
3. **Opening** in Ruby's voice: a scene or a plain statement, not "In this article we will".
4. **H2s** that answer the questions parents actually type. Short paragraphs. One list at most.
5. **A "from the pool" box**: one specific thing Ruby has seen (no child identified).
6. **CTA to the free taster** at the end, plus one in-line link to the relevant programme in the body.
7. **Author line:** "Ruby Waller is a qualified swimming teacher and competitive swimmer with Maidstone SC. She teaches 1:1 and small-group lessons in Sevenoaks through RW Swim Academy." **[CONFIRM qualification wording and club]**
8. **Suggested visuals:** 1 to 3, listed at the end of each draft, for Chris to source.
