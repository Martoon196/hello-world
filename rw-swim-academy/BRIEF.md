## 3. Who we're for (ideal customer profiles)

**ICP 1 — "Confidence parents" (primary, 60% of revenue)**
Parents in Sevenoaks / Tonbridge / Tunbridge Wells / Westerham / Otford / Kemsing with children aged 4–10 who are nervous, plateaued in council group lessons (10 kids, 1 teacher, no progress for two terms) or need to pass school swimming. Mums do most of the researching and booking. They buy on trust, safety, warmth, and *visible progress*. They hang out in local Facebook groups, school WhatsApp groups, Mumsnet, Instagram.

**ICP 2 — "Club-track parents" (secondary, 30%)**
Parents of 8–14 year olds who are in a club or want to get in, want a stroke-technique edge, or want a role model who's done it. They care about technique, times, and pathway knowledge. Ruby's competitive record is the whole pitch here.

**ICP 3 — "Sibling / friend pairs and adults" (10%)**
Two siblings or friends wanting a shared lesson; a handful of adult improvers/triathletes later. Not marketed to at launch, but priced.
