The small print, in plain English

Where

Sevenoaks and surrounding areas. Your venue is confirmed when you book. A lifeguard is always on poolside.

Moving a lesson

24 hours' notice to move a lesson, one move per block. Less than 24 hours and the lesson is lost. Ill on the day? Message before 8am and we'll try to swap.

Paying

Card payment online when you book, handled by Stripe. Blocks are paid up front. Founding members get 10% off automatically.
