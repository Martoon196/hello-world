How it works

Booking to certificate, step by step

Everything you need to know before your child's first lesson.

  1. Book a free taster online

    Choose a time that suits you. You'll get a confirmation email with the venue, what to bring and where to park. Tasters are limited to six a week.

  2. Taster day

    Arrive 10 minutes early. Ruby spends 20 minutes in the water with your child: some games, some floating, a few widths if they're ready. You watch from poolside. Then 5 minutes with you.

  3. Your written assessment

    Within 24 hours you get an email with your child's level (Splash, Stroke or Sprint), the skills they have, the skills to work on next, and Ruby's recommendation. If she thinks they'd be fine in group lessons elsewhere, she'll say so.

  4. Book a block or programme

    Choose a weekly slot online and pay by card. Founding members get 10% off automatically. You'll get a calendar invite for every lesson.

  5. Lessons, with progress you can see

    Same day, same time, every week. Ruby sends a short progress note at week 4 and at the end of the block, with skills ticked off. Programmes end with a certificate.

  6. Renew, move up, or move on

    Two weeks before a block ends, Ruby holds your slot and emails you a renewal link. Move up a level, switch to a programme, or stop. No contracts.

The three levels

LevelTypical ageWhat it means
Splash4–6Water confidence: face in, floating, first kicks and pushes, jumping in and returning to the side.
Stroke6–10Technique: front crawl with breathing, backstroke, breaststroke timing, 25m+ with good form.
Sprint9–14Club-ready: all four strokes, starts, turns, pacing, race craft.

What to bring

  • Swimsuit or jammers (nothing baggy)
  • Goggles that fit (Ruby can help you choose)
  • Swim hat for long hair
  • Towel, and a warm layer for after
  • A snack for the car. Swimming makes them hungry.

What to expect from us

  • A confirmation email and calendar invite for every lesson
  • A lifeguard on poolside at every session
  • A written progress note every block
  • A reply to any message within one working day
