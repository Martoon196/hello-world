What we collect, why, and your rights. Last updated September 2026.

Who we are

RW Swim Academy, Sevenoaks, Kent. Data controller: [CONFIRM legal entity name]. Contact: hello@rwswimacademy.co.uk.

What we collect

  • Parent or carer name, email, phone.
  • Child's first name, age, swimming ability, and any medical or additional needs you tell us about (special category data, used only to keep your child safe, with your consent).
  • Booking and payment records. Card details are handled by Stripe and never stored by us.
  • Photo and video consent choices.
  • Website form submissions and, if you join the waiting list, marketing preferences.

Why we use it

  • To deliver and manage lessons (contract).
  • To keep children safe (legal obligation and consent).
  • To send you lesson updates (contract) and, if you opt in, news and offers (consent; unsubscribe any time).

Who we share it with

Stripe (payments), Google Calendar (scheduling), our email provider (confirmations and newsletters), and our website host (Netlify). We never sell your data.

How long we keep it

Booking and medical records for 3 years after your last lesson (insurance requirement), then deleted. Waiting list data until you unsubscribe or 2 years of inactivity.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent, by emailing us. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
