Meet your coach

Hi, I'm Ruby.

I've been swimming competitively since I was small, and I'm now a qualified swimming teacher training towards the Olympic pathway. I started RW Swim Academy because I remember exactly what it felt like to be scared of putting my face in the water, and exactly which coach made it click.

I teach in Sevenoaks and across west Kent, in small groups or one to one, and I keep it simple: confidence first, then technique, then speed.

How it works

From nervous to swimming in three steps

  1. Book a free taster

    Pick a time online. Ruby spends 20 minutes in the water with your child and 5 minutes with you.

  2. Get an honest assessment

    You get a written level (Splash, Stroke or Sprint) and Ruby's recommendation. Sometimes that's "they're fine in group lessons".

  3. Book a block and watch them go

    Same day, same time, every week. Progress notes at week 4 and 6, and your slot is held for next term.

What parents will get

We're new. Here's what we promise instead of reviews.

RW Swim Academy opens in January 2027, so we don't have a wall of testimonials yet. What we can promise:

  • The first-lesson promise. If your child doesn't want to come back after lesson one of a block, tell us and we refund the rest. No awkward conversation.
  • Written progress, every block. Not "she did well today". Actual skills, ticked off.
  • A coach who turns up. Ruby's training schedule is built into the booking calendar, so lessons don't get cancelled for a gala.
  • Safeguarding done properly. Enhanced DBS, Swim England safeguarding training, insured, a lifeguard always on poolside, and nothing posted online without your written consent.
