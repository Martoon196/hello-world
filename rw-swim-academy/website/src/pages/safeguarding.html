Safeguarding

Keeping children safe in the water and out of it

This is the bit we take most seriously.

Our commitments

  • Every RW Swim Academy teacher holds an enhanced DBS check with children's barred list check, renewed every three years, and Swim England safeguarding training.
  • A qualified lifeguard is on poolside at every session. We do not teach at venues without one.
  • No group is larger than four swimmers. Under-8s must have a parent or carer at the venue for the whole session.
  • Teachers never get changed with children, never transport children, and never message a child directly. All communication goes to parents.
  • Photos and video are only taken with written consent, never show names, venues or lesson times, and can be withdrawn at any time.
  • Medical information and emergency contacts are collected at booking, stored securely, and only used to keep your child safe.

Raising a concern

If you are worried about a child's welfare at any RW Swim Academy session, tell us straight away: hello@rwswimacademy.co.uk, or speak to the venue's duty manager. If a child is in immediate danger, call 999. You can also contact the NSPCC helpline on 0808 800 5000 or Swim England's safeguarding team.
