Terms & conditions

Plain English. Last updated September 2026.

Starting point only. Have these checked by a solicitor or your insurer before launch. See business/06-templates.md for the fuller version.

1. Who we are

RW Swim Academy ("we", "us") provides swimming lessons in and around Sevenoaks, Kent. Contact: hello@rwswimacademy.co.uk.

2. Booking and payment

Bookings are made online and confirmed by email. Blocks, programmes, intensives and clinics are paid in full at booking by card through Stripe. Single lessons are paid at booking. Prices include VAT where applicable.

3. Moving or cancelling a lesson

You may move one lesson per block to another available slot with at least 24 hours' notice. Lessons cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice are charged in full, because the pool time is already paid for. If your child is unwell on the day, message us before 8am and we will try to reschedule, though we cannot guarantee it.

4. The first-lesson promise

If, after the first lesson of a block or programme, your child does not wish to continue, tell us within 48 hours and we will refund the remaining lessons in full.

5. Cancellation by us

If we cancel a lesson (for example pool closure or teacher illness) we will offer a replacement lesson or a refund for that lesson.

6. Your right to cancel

Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 you have 14 days from booking to cancel for a full refund, unless lessons have already started, in which case we refund the lessons not yet delivered.

7. Health and safety

You must tell us about any medical condition, allergy or additional need that could affect your child in the water. A parent or carer must remain at the venue for children under 8. You must follow the venue's rules.

8. Photos and video

We only take or publish images of your child with your written consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

9. Liability

Nothing in these terms limits our liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence. We are not responsible for loss or damage to personal belongings at venues.

10. Data

We handle your data as described in our privacy notice.

11. Changes

We may update these terms; the version on this page at the time of booking applies.
