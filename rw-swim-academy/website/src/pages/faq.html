FAQ

Questions parents ask

If yours isn't here, send it over and we'll answer within a working day.

Getting started

What age can my child start?

From 4. Under-4s do best in parent-and-child water play rather than lessons, so we'd suggest waiting. There's no upper age limit; Ruby also coaches teenagers and, by enquiry, adults.

Is the taster really free?

Yes. 20 minutes in the water, a written assessment, and Ruby's recommendation. No card details, no obligation. We limit tasters to six a week so they don't eat into lesson time, which is why they book up.

My child is terrified of water. Is a taster still a good idea?

Especially then. Ruby was that child. The taster might be twenty minutes of sitting on the side kicking, and that's fine. Nothing happens until your child is ready for it.

Where are the lessons?

In hired lanes at pools in and around Sevenoaks. Your venue is confirmed when you book and appears in your calendar invite. A qualified lifeguard is on duty at every venue we use.

Do you teach in private home pools?

Yes, by enquiry, within about 20 minutes of Sevenoaks. A travel charge applies and the pool must meet our safety requirements. Ask us.

Lessons and levels

Private or group: which is better?

Private (1:1) is fastest for nervous swimmers, stroke correction and deadlines. Programmes (max 4) are better value, add a bit of friendly competition and suit children who are already comfortable in the water. Ruby will recommend after the taster. Longer answer here.

How quickly will my child progress?

Honestly, it depends on age, confidence and how often they swim. As a guide, most 5–7 year olds who start in Splash are swimming a width unaided within a block of 6, and most Stroke swimmers add 10 metres of good front crawl per block. You'll see it in the progress notes either way.

Can two children share a lesson?

Yes, if they're within one level of each other. It's our semi-private option: £270 for a block of six, £22.50 per swimmer per lesson.

What does "training towards the Olympic pathway" actually mean?

Ruby competes at county, regional and national qualifier level and is working through the British Swimming performance pathway. We say "pathway" rather than "Olympian" because we'd rather be accurate than impressive.

Booking, paying, cancelling

How do I pay?

By card online when you book, through Stripe. Blocks and programmes are paid up front. We don't store your card details.

What if we need to miss a lesson?

Give us 24 hours' notice and we'll move one lesson per block to another slot. With less than 24 hours' notice the lesson is lost, because the lane is already paid for. If your child is ill on the day, message before 8am and we'll do our best to swap.

What if my child doesn't like it?

The first-lesson promise: if they don't want to come back after the first lesson of a block, tell us and we'll refund the remaining lessons. No awkward conversation.

What happens if the pool closes?

If we cancel (pool closure, Ruby unwell), you get the lesson added to the end of your block or a refund for that lesson, your choice.

What is a founding member?

One of the first 30 swimmers to join. They get 10% off every block they ever book, a free RW swim cap and first pick of slots each term. Join the list.

Safety and safeguarding

What checks does Ruby have?

Enhanced DBS with children's barred list check, Swim England safeguarding training, a recognised swimming teaching qualification, and public liability and professional indemnity insurance. Certificates are available to view on request.

Is a lifeguard present?

Always. We only use venues with a qualified lifeguard on poolside during our sessions.

Can I stay and watch?

Yes, and for under-8s we ask that you do. Poolside or viewing gallery, depending on the venue.

Will photos of my child appear online?

Only with your written consent, given at booking and changeable at any time. We never post names, venues or lesson times alongside images of children.
