# RW Swim Academy — the complete launch pack

Everything needed to take Ruby Waller's swim school from an idea in a WhatsApp group to a paying business in Sevenoaks: the strategy, the offers and pricing, the legal and operational checklist, a 90-day plan, a designed website with online booking, card payments and calendar sync, and a full marketing kit (social, email, blog, ads, local outreach, press).

**Start here:** [`BRIEF.md`](BRIEF.md) — the one-page brand and offer bible everything else is built from. Then read [`business/01-launch-strategy.md`](business/01-launch-strategy.md).

Anything marked **[CONFIRM]** anywhere in this folder is a placeholder or an unverified fact that Chris or Ruby must check before it goes public (qualification name, current club, venue, prices once lane hire is known).

## The plan in one paragraph

Do not build a pool. Build demand. Ruby proves the offer herself in hired lanes in Sevenoaks, with a free taster as the front door and a 30-swimmer founding-member launch in January 2027. Every block comes with a written assessment and progress notes, so the business has proof (retention, reviews, a waiting list, profitable sessions) before anyone talks about investors, coaches or industrial units. Stage 2 (first extra coach) only when Ruby is full. Stage 4 (a facility) only with hundreds of paying swimmers and a lane-access constraint.

### `website/` — the site, booking, payments, calendar
A designed, mobile-first website with a four-step booking wizard: choose lesson → pick a time from Ruby's live Google Calendar availability → swimmer details → pay by card through Stripe (free tasters skip payment). After payment, every lesson is written to Ruby's calendar with the parent invited, and confirmation emails go to both sides. Waiting-list and contact forms are built in. Blog posts are Markdown files. Prices and Ruby's hours are two small JSON files.

See [`website/README.md`](website/README.md) for the afternoon-long "go live" guide (Netlify, domain, Stripe, Google Calendar, email) and day-to-day editing.
